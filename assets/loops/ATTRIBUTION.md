# The bundled guitar loops

These recordings are not under this repository's GPL-3.0. They are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as the dataset's Zenodo record states ([https://zenodo.org/records/14963133](https://zenodo.org/records/14963133), read on 2026-11-05). All are the dry direct-input channel of the guitar, with no amp.

The dataset's authors ask users to cite their paper: Guitar-TECHS, ICASSP 2025, doi:10.1109/ICASSP49660.2025.10887996.

`prepare.py` records how each file was made. BPM values come from the dataset's MIDI files.

- `chug.wav`: "Low riff", cut from Guitar-TECHS (directinput_02.wav of P3_music) by Hegel Emmanuel Pedroza Villalobos, Termeh Taheri, Wallace Abreu, Ryan Corey and Iran R. Roman, https://zenodo.org/records/14963133, CC BY 4.0. Changed: cut to a loop, crossfaded, resampled to 44.1 kHz, converted to mono 16-bit, normalised.
